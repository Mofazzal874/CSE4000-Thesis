# Editorial basis and external references

Checked on 2026-08-16.

## Authorship detectors

Turnitin's own guide says its AI-writing model can misidentify human-written,
AI-generated, and AI-paraphrased text. It also says the score should not be the sole
basis for adverse action and suppresses exact scores below 20% because false positives
are more likely in that range:

- https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/22774058814093-Using-the-AI-Writing-Report

For that reason this review does not claim that a detector will return zero, and it
does not edit towards a detector. The review criteria are traceability, technical
accuracy, proportionate claims, readable sentence structure, and consistent layout.

Only structural habits were taken from these sources: state the problem before the
method, place evidence next to the claim it supports, distinguish measurement from
interpretation, and make limitations specific. No prose was copied.
